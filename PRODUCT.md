# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Adult learners practicing Korean on iOS and Android, including people preparing to live, study or work in Korea. They need short, approachable speaking sessions and useful explanations in English. The existing research identifies confidence, relevant situations and repetition as the core learning loop.

## Product Purpose
Haru helps a learner choose a real-life situation, speak Korean with an AI tutor, understand a correction, and review useful expressions later. This MVP uses Expo, Supabase and Gemini. The latest implementation brief replaces earlier web-first architecture proposals. Success means completing this loop with real authenticated state and secure speech integrations.

## Brand Personality
Warm, composed, encouraging. Haru is an original product, with an panda tutor named Dori, animated in Rive from the supplied vector rig. Its voice invites another attempt without making every sentence a test.

## Anti-references
No copied Kigaru branding, illustrations or interface. No complex 3D world, noisy game dashboard, streak guilt, decorative glass, fake activity, simulated AI conversations or mystery pronunciation scores.

## Design Principles
Speaking is the main action. Show the audio lifecycle plainly. Explain one useful correction at a time. Let users read and explore lessons before configuration. Save learning evidence, not invented mastery. Never hide missing service configuration behind fabricated responses.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable Korean text, labeled controls, generous touch targets, reduced-motion support, optional translations, keyboard/text fallback and clear microphone-permission handling. Color must not be the only state indicator. Support both iOS and Android with safe-area-aware layouts.
